Best Movies of 2026

Top 5 Movies

Dhurandhar: The Revenge

Genre: Action, Thriller

Director: Rajesh Kumar

I liked the action sequences and the performances of the lead actors.

Project Hail Mary

Genre: Sci-Fi, Adventure

Director: Andy Weir

The storyline was gripping and the visual effects were stunning.

I enjoyed the humanity angle explored in the film.

Remarkably Bright Creatures

Genre: Drama, Mystery

Director: Shelby Van Pelt

The film was a feel good movie about human endurance and the power of connection.

Happy Patel

Genre: Comedy, Drama

Director: Rajkumar Hirani

The film was hilarious and had great amount of puns and witty comedy about some Indian stereotypes perceived by the western world.

Love Insurance Kompany

Genre: Romantic Comedy

Director: Anurag Basu

The film was a light-hearted romantic comedy with a unique concept of love insurance. It had great chemistry between the lead actors and a heartwarming storyline.
